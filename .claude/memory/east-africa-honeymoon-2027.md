## 2026-06-14 — decision: October 2027 chosen as travel window
User selected October 2027 from three candidate windows (June, October, Feb). Reasoning: 20–30% below peak pricing, thin dry vegetation for easy wildlife sightings, migration herds returning south through northern Serengeti, Zanzibar nearly empty with exceptional snorkeling (28–29°C, 20–30m visibility). Target window: Oct 5–19 — avoid Oct 20+ where short rains risk increases meaningfully.

## 2026-06-14 — learning: No crater-rim camp has crater views from inside the tent — universal Ngorongoro geography
All crater-rim camps at Ngorongoro sit in highland forest on the caldera edge. The crater is below; tents face forest, not open caldera. Views only occur on game drives when descending. Only permanent lodges built into the crater wall (Serena, Meliá/Wildlife Lodge) offer room-level views — of the exterior rim, not the crater interior. This surprised the user in planning; always clarify before recommending canvas camps for the "views" benefit.

## 2026-06-14 — learning: Maasai displacement at Ngorongoro is an active human rights situation (as of 2026)
Coercive displacement of ~82,000–100,000 Maasai documented 2022–2024 by HRW. Arusha High Court suspended further evictions Aug 2024. Presidential acknowledgment + commissions formed Dec 2024. Commission reports presented March 2026, disputed by Maasai lawyer as "copy-paste state propaganda." Government/NCAA lodge (now Meliá Collection) eliminated — revenues fund the same NCAA body overseeing displacement. Top ethical operators: Entamanu/Nomad Tanzania (KopeLion lion guardians, Maasai nursery, concrete-free, Tanzanian-owned), Lemala (Lerai Foundation). Avoid: Thomson Safaris/Lindblad (documented Maasai land rights violations since 2006).

## 2026-06-14 — decision: Serengeti camp positioning for October = Northern Serengeti (Kogatende area)
Migration herds return south through northern Serengeti in October. All camp searches should target Kogatende/Bolongonja corridor, NOT Central/Southern Serengeti. Wayo Green Camp North: value leader at ~$240/pp/night all-in including drinks, 8–10 tents, intimate. Ang'ata Migration Camp: better honeymoon infrastructure (bush dinners standard, night drives), $350–500/pp. Ethical credentials of Wayo not yet verified — required before finalizing. Balloon safaris: Serengeti Balloon Safaris ($599/pp shared, private charter ~$3,500–4,000 total); book 6+ months in advance; October is tail end of dry-season balloon window.

## 2026-06-14 — decision: Zanzibar east coast preferred over north coast
Mnemba Atoll (best snorkeling in Zanzibar) is 15–20 min boat from Matemwe (east coast) vs 40–60 min from Nungwi/Kendwa (north coast). East coast gives Sarah quieter/authentic vibe AND gives Nir better snorkeling access — both priorities served.
