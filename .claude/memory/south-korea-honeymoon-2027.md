## 2026-06-27 — learning: Key SK logistics — Hallasan reservation + Busan→Jeju is a flight

Two non-obvious logistics items confirmed by research:
1. **Hallasan summit requires advance reservation** (free, but capped at 500–1,000 hikers/day). Book at k-hike.kr on the 1st of the month for the following month. Seongpanak trail (9.6km, 1,000/day cap) or Gwaneumsa trail (8.7km, 500/day cap — more dramatic scenery). Failure to book = no summit.
2. **Busan → Jeju is a flight, not a train** — Jeju is an island. Air Busan flies PUS→CJU nonstop in ~55 min, ~$40/person booked in advance. Book directly from Busan airport; do NOT backtrack to Seoul for the Gimpo→Jeju route. At end of trip: CJU→ICN domestic flight needed (~$40–80/person) to connect to international ORD return.
**How to apply:** When building the itinerary, include the PUS→CJU flight as a separate booking item. Flag Hallasan reservation timing in any pre-trip checklist.

## 2026-07-03 — learning: Gyeongju day trip — logistics, sites, costs confirmed

**Cherry blossom peak: March 27–April 5** (based on 2025 data: first bloom March 23, full peak March 30; ±5–7 day variance year to year). Day B2 (March 27, Saturday) opens peak. Arrive early to beat weekend crowds. **Transport:** Subway Line 2→Line 1 from Haeundae to Busan Station (~40 min, ₩1,700) or taxi (~$20) → KTX to Singyeongju, 28–33 min, ~₩9,000–11,000/person. Book in advance at [letskorail.com](https://www.letskorail.com) — cherry blossom Saturday seats sell out. From Singyeongju: taxi to Bulguksa ~20 min ~$12; Bus 700 covers main sites in sequence. **Sites:** Bulguksa Temple (불국사, free since May 2023, 90 min, arrive before 9 AM) → Seokguram Grotto (석굴암, ₩5,000/person, 45 min, taxi up mountain) → Tumuli Park / Daereungwon (대릉원, free since May 2023, 90 min, enter Cheonmachong tomb ₩500–2,000) → lunch Hwangnidan-gil (황리단길, ~$18–22/2) + Cheomseongdae Observatory (첨성대, free, 15 min, 5-min walk from Tumuli) → Wolji Pond / Donggung Palace (동궁과 월지, ₩3,000/person, 90 min, best at dusk). Taxi to Singyeongju ~$10, KTX back 5:30 PM, arrive Busan ~6 PM. **Full day cost for 2:** ~$115–130 all-in (transport + admissions + food).
