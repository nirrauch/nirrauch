**Trip:** Nir & Sarah Honeymoon · March–April 2027
**Researched:** 2026-06-06
**Conversion rate used:** NZD 1 = USD 0.60

### Ben Lomond Hike
| | |
|---|---|
| **Cost** | **FREE** — self-guided |

## WĀNAKA (45 min from Queenstown)

### Roys Peak Hike
| | |
|---|---|
| **Cost** | **FREE** — self-guided via DOC |
| **Distance** | 16km return · 1,578m summit |

## BOOKING NOTES

- **Book as soon as dates are confirmed:** Nevis Bungee, Milford fly-cruise-fly, Tongariro shuttle (or guide), Dolphin Encounter, Whale Watch all sell out for March.
- **Prefer direct booking** over Viator/GetYourGuide where possible — often cheaper and easier to modify.
- **Weather-dependent activities:** Milford Sound flights, heli-hike, skydiving — always book a buffer day before departure.
- **March timing advantage:** Lower crowds than peak Jan–Feb, still great weather, many activities at non-peak pricing.
