Highlights

Tongariro Alpine Crossing
New Zealand's most iconic day hike — 20km across an active volcanic plateau. Red Crater, emerald alpine lakes, ash fields, and a summit at 1,886m. Challenging, unforgettable, and deeply surreal.
$33/person (shuttle) · $177+ guided
↗
Milford Sound: Fly-Cruise-Fly
Forty minutes over Fiordland's snowcapped peaks by light plane, then a 1h45m cruise through the fjord past waterfalls and resident seals. The return flight over the Southern Alps seals it as the most spectacular day in the country.
$435–$609/person
↗
Nevis Bungee — 134m
The highest bungy jump in New Zealand: a 134-metre drop off a cable car suspended above the Nevis River gorge, with 8.5 seconds of freefall. Not a tourist gimmick — this is the real thing.
$237/person
↗
Rotorua: Māori Culture
Te Puia's working Māori arts institute erupts beside the Pōhutu Geyser. The Whakarewarewa living village is exactly that — families still cook in geothermal pools. One of the few places in the world where ancient culture is genuinely alive, not performed.
$72/person (Te Puia) · $24/person (village)
↗
Lomani Island, Fiji
Seventeen rooms on a private patch of Malolo Lailai Island — adults-only, intimate enough that staff learn your names on day one. Daily guided reef snorkel trips, kava ceremonies, and evening beach dinners. The post-adventure exhale.
From $358/night · 3 nights
↗
Wānaka + Roys Peak
Forty-five minutes from Queenstown, Wānaka is quieter, prettier, and completely different in character. Roys Peak is a 5-hour return summit hike with one of the most photographed panoramas in New Zealand — the Southern Alps and Lake Wānaka from 1,578m.
Free hike · Rippon vineyard tasting from free
↗
Central Otago Wine Harvest
March–April is harvest season in the Gibbston Valley — the world's southernmost Pinot Noir region. Rolling schist canyon, boutique cellar doors, and vineyard lunches that stretch into the afternoon.
$141–$171/person (half-day tour with lunch)
↗
Walter Peak Dinner Cruise
Board the vintage TSS Earnslaw steamship at Queenstown's pier, cross Lake Wakatipu to the high-country Walter Peak farm, and sit down to a gourmet BBQ dinner as the Remarkables turn pink at sunset.
$119/person

Day by Day

Day 1 Auckland Arrival
~$50/couple
☀️ Morning
Long-haul arrival from Chicago. Clear customs, grab the rental car. Coffee from a good Auckland café — flat whites are a different league here.
🌤 Afternoon
Check into the transit hotel near the waterfront. Walk the Viaduct Harbour. Light grocery run for snacks and tomorrow's road trip provisions.
🌙 Evening
Early dinner — something simple and local. Early night. The adventure starts properly tomorrow.
Day 2 Auckland → Rotorua Into the Geothermal Zone
~$200/couple
☀️ Morning
Hit the road early — 2.5 hours south to Rotorua. Stop in Tirau for flat whites and the world's most surreal small-town corrugated-iron buildings.
🌤 Afternoon
Wai-O-Tapu Thermal Landscape — the Champagne Pool (vivid orange silica edges), Artist's Palette (green and yellow sulphur formations), Lady Knox Geyser. 2–3 hours self-guided. $27/person. 30 minutes from Rotorua centre.
🌙 Evening
Polynesian Spa — 12 geothermal mineral pools set over Lake Rotorua, some dating back to 1882. Deluxe Double Dipper includes both the historic Pavilion pools and the cliff-edge Lake Spa pools. $66/person. Book the 5pm–7pm slot for golden hour.
Day 3 Rotorua: Māori Culture Culture & Living History
~$290/couple
☀️ Morning
Te Puia — the working Māori arts institute. Watch master carvers and weavers at work, then catch the 90-minute guided experience including the Pōhutu Geyser and a cultural performance (haka, poi, waiata). $72/person. Book the 9am slot to beat tour groups.
🌤 Afternoon
Whakarewarewa Living Village — unlike Te Puia, this village is still fully inhabited. Guides are residents. Families cook in the thermal pools. 1-hour guided tour. $24/person. This is the more authentic of the two — fewer theatrics, more real.
🌙 Evening
Dinner on the Rotorua waterfront strip. Try Atticus Finch for craft beer and shared plates, or Eat Streat for local flavour and open-air atmosphere.
Day 4 Rotorua → Taupo Volcanic Heartland
~$100/couple
☀️ Morning
Pack up and drive south toward Taupo (1 hour). Stop at Huka Falls — the most visited natural site in New Zealand and genuinely worth it. A turquoise, 220,000-litre-per-second rush through a narrow canyon. Free, 5-minute walk from the car park.
🌤 Afternoon
Check into Tongariro Crossing Lodge. Early to bed is optional — the better option is the lakeside Taupo dining strip for a long, relaxed dinner.
🌙 Evening
Early dinner, early sleep. The shuttle departs at 6:30am.
Day 5 Tongariro Alpine Crossing The Big One
~$70/couple
☀️ Morning
6:30am shuttle from the lodge drops you at Mangatepopo trailhead. The opening hour crosses a vast lava field toward the volcanoes — Mt Ngauruhoe rising on your right (the original Mt Doom). Red Crater summit at 1,886m.
🌤 Afternoon
The descent from Red Crater passes the glowing emerald Emerald Lakes — thermal-tinted and completely surreal at altitude. 20.2km total, 6–8 hours. Shuttle picks you up from Ketetahi end by 3pm.
🌙 Evening
Back at the lodge by 4pm. Into the spa pool immediately. Dinner in the village. Legs will complain. That's correct.
Day 6 Travel to Queenstown Adventure Capital Awaits
~$300/couple
☀️ Morning
Drive back toward Auckland (or Rotorua Airport). Domestic flight to Queenstown — 1h20m, with aerial views of the North Island from above. Air New Zealand domestic is reliable.
🌤 Afternoon
Arrive Queenstown. Drop bags at Kamana Lakehouse — the Remarkables visible from check-in. Walk the waterfront along Lake Wakatipu.
🌙 Evening
Fergburger. Yes, the queue. Yes, it's worth it. Craft beer at Altitude Brewing after. Early night — tomorrow is one of the best days of the trip.
Day 7 Milford Sound: Fly-Cruise-Fly The Most Spectacular Day in New Zealand
~$870/couple
☀️ Morning
Queenstown Airport, 8am flight over Fiordland. The aerial approach to Milford Sound — snowcapped peaks, glacial valleys, no roads below — is itself worth the price. Land at Milford aerodrome.
🌤 Afternoon
1h45m cruise through the fjord. Stirling Falls up close, fur seals on the rocks, bottlenose dolphins if the weather behaves. The scale is incomprehensible in photos — you understand it only from the water.
🌙 Evening
Return flight over the Southern Alps. Back in Queenstown by 3pm. Dinner at a waterfront restaurant — Finz Seafood & Grill for a blow-out, or Madam Woo for something more relaxed.
Day 8 Adventure Day All-In
~$690/couple
☀️ Morning
Nevis Bungee, 8am slot. 134m platform suspended from a cable car above the Nevis Gorge. 8.5 seconds of freefall. Includes return transfer from Queenstown, photo package, and certificate. $237/person.
🌤 Afternoon
Dart River Wilderness Jet Boat — 90 minutes, up to 90km through Mt Aspiring National Park. 80mph through canyon walls on a river where Lord of the Rings was filmed. $106/person.
🌙 Evening
TSS Earnslaw and the Walter Peak Dinner Cruise. Board the 1912 vintage coal-fired steamship at the Queenstown pier, cross Lake Wakatipu, gourmet BBQ at the high-country farm, back by 9:30pm. $119/person. Book ahead — sells out.
Day 9 Wānaka Alpine Serenity
~$70/couple
☀️ Morning
45-minute drive to Wānaka. Arrive by 7am for Roys Peak — 5–6 hours return, 1,578m summit, the most iconic panorama in the South Island. Free. Trekking poles useful.
🌤 Afternoon
Descend into Wānaka town. Rippon Vineyard cellar door tasting — 4–6 Pinot Noir wines on a lakeside deck with the peak behind you, by appointment. From free.
🌙 Evening
Drive back to Queenstown (45 min) in time for dinner. Try Rata for a celebratory South Island meal.
Day 10 Wine Country Harvest Season
~$290/couple
☀️ Morning
Central Otago half-day wine tour. Gibbston Valley schist canyon, 3–4 boutique cellar doors, vineyard lunch included. March is harvest — you may walk the rows during picking. $141/person.
🌤 Afternoon
Back in Queenstown by 3pm. Lake walk, final afternoon in the adventure capital. Pack for Fiji.
🌙 Evening
Celebratory last-night-in-NZ dinner. Botswana Butchery on the waterfront — South Island rack of lamb.
Day 11 Fly to Fiji The Pacific Begins

Fly Queenstown → Nadi via Auckland. Total journey time approximately 6–7 hours including the connection.
🌤 Afternoon

🌙 Evening
Explore the 17-room resort, which takes about 15 minutes. Watch the sunset over the lagoon from the beach. The pace has officially changed.

Kava ceremony with staff — a genuine Fijian cultural exchange, not a staged show. Dinner on the beach.
