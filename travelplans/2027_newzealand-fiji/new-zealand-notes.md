## Confirmed NZ Stops

### ✅ Rotorua
**Why confirmed:** Nir interested in Māori history and culture.
**What's here:** Te Puia (geysers + working Māori arts institute), Wai-O-Tapu thermal landscape (vivid silica terraces and colored pools), Whakarewarewa living village (residents cook in geothermal pools), Polynesian Spa (hot pools over Lake Rotorua).
**Suggested stay:** 2 nights
**Nearest airport:** Rotorua (ROT) or drive from Auckland (2.5h)

### ✅ Taupo + Tongariro
**Why confirmed:** Tongariro Alpine Crossing confirmed as a target activity. Nir drawn to the volcanic drama.

#### Tongariro Alpine Crossing — Full Research Summary
- **Type:** Point-to-point day hike — NOT a loop. Starts at Mangatepopo Road End, finishes at Ketetahi Road End (~20km apart).
- **Distance:** 20.2km · **Elevation gain:** ~800m · **High point:** 1,886m (Red Crater) · **Duration:** 6–8 hours
- **Difficulty:** Moderate–Difficult (DOC rating). Doable for regularly active hikers. No technical scrambling — main challenge is endurance and the steep loose-scree descent from Red Crater. Trekking poles strongly recommended.
- **Can you get back to a hotel the same day?** YES. Shuttle picks you up from your accommodation (~15–25 min from trailhead), drops at Mangatepopo start. After finishing at Ketetahi, shuttle returns you to your hotel. You sleep in the same bed both nights. Shuttle cost ~NZD $45–55/person (~$30 USD). Operators: [Tongariro Crossing Shuttles](https://tongarirocrossingshuttles.co.nz/), [Summit Shuttles](https://www.summitshuttles.co.nz/).
- **Best timing:** March (ideal — good weather, 30–50% fewer crowds than peak). April (good, slightly cooler). May (requires guide + crampons — avoid independently).
- **Guided option (recommended for honeymoon):** [Walk into Luxury](https://www.walkintoluxury.com/experiences/tongariro-alpine-crossing-guided-hike) — private guided crossing, couples-friendly, sets own pace, ~NZD $250–295/person. Also: [Adventure Outdoors Tongariro](https://www.adventureoutdoors.co.nz), [Tongariro Guided Walks](https://www.tongariroguidedwalks.nz/).
- **Sarah caveat:** Sarah's pace profile suggests she may resist a 6:30am shuttle commitment. Options: (a) both do it with private guide, set own pace; (b) Nir does the crossing while Sarah has a spa day at the lodge — reunite for dinner. Decide before booking.
- **Recovery:** Plan for a hot pools / spa day in Taupo after the crossing. Legs will be sore.
- **Nearby accommodation:** Tongariro Crossing Lodge ([tongarirocrossinglodge.co.nz](https://www.tongarirocrossinglodge.co.nz/)), Turangi (~15 min from trailhead finish, wider amenity range).

**Other Taupo highlights:** Skydiving over Lake Taupo (Nir's profile — fits perfectly), Huka Falls, lakeside dining strip.
**Suggested stay:** 2 nights (1 pre-hike, 1 recovery)

### ✅ Queenstown
**Why confirmed:** Best match for Nir's profile. "Adventure Capital of the World." Non-negotiable.
**What's here:**
- **Nevis Bungee** — 134m, 8.5 sec freefall, highest in NZ ([aj-hackett.com](https://www.aj-hackett.com/en-nz/nevis-bungy/))
- **Milford Sound** — 4h drive away; do as a day trip (fly-cruise-fly ~half day) or overnight cruise. Unmissable.
- **Jet boating** — Dart River, 90mph through canyon walls
- **Heli-hiking / heli-skiing** — private helicopter drop into backcountry
- **Canyoning** — rappelling waterfalls, swimming gorges
- **Central Otago wine** — world-class Pinot Noir, vineyard lunches
- **Ben Lomond hike** — 5h summit hike, insane views (no guide needed, just fitness)
- **Fergburger** — absurd wait, worth it
- **Walter Peak dinner cruise** — romantic, high-country farm across the lake
- **Wānaka** (45 min away) — quieter sibling to Queenstown, Roys Peak hike, Rippon vineyard, Rob Roy glacier. Good for 1 night of genuine quiet.
- **SKIP per Nir:** Ziplines and "mild thrill" tourist activities
**Suggested stay:** 3–4 nights (base for Milford Sound day trip + Wānaka overnight option)
**Airport:** Queenstown (ZQN) — consider flying in from Auckland rather than driving the full South Island

### ⭐ Kaikōura (Bonus Stop — not in main itinerary)
**Status:** Moved to bonus. The routing from Queenstown → Fiji doesn't naturally pass through Kaikōura (it's north of CHC, requiring a detour). Included as an optional add-on if dates allow.
**What's here:** Sperm whale watching tours (95% success rate, ~$105/person), swim-with-Dusky-dolphins ($144/person), fresh crayfish — one of the best seafood meals in NZ, seal colony coastal walks.
**If adding:** Fly QZN → CHC, rent car, drive north to Kaikōura (2.5h), whale watch + lunch, drive back to CHC, fly CHC → NAN next morning. Adds 1 day and ~$400–500 per couple (car + whale watch).
**Accommodation if staying:** Hapuku Lodge Tree Houses (~$500–700/night all-in) — genuinely worth it if staying overnight.

## Locations Researched — Not Confirmed

### Auckland — Transit Only
No reason to linger. Use as entry/exit point. **Waiheke Island** (40min ferry) is worth an overnight if time allows — vineyards, beaches, excellent restaurants, completely different vibe from the city. Otherwise 1 night max.

### Wellington — Possible Transit
Good food city, Te Papa museum (excellent free national museum), Cuba Street bars. Worth 1–2 nights if routing through on the ferry. Not a destination on its own given trip length.

### Bay of Islands — Low Priority
Beautiful, culturally significant (Treaty of Waitangi), but adds a full day north of Auckland. Skip given time constraints unless routing allows it without effort.

### Abel Tasman / Nelson — Not Confirmed
Golden beaches, sea kayaking, NZ's sunniest region. Good alternative if Fiji falls through for beach component. Not necessary given Fiji is in the plan.

### Christchurch — Transit Base
Main South Island airport (CHC). Good food scene, interesting post-earthquake rebuild. Worth 1 evening if flying in/out. Not a destination — a gateway.

### Franz Josef Glacier — On Hold
West Coast glaciers, dramatic scenery. Adds significant driving time (5h from Queenstown). Worth it only if routing allows — would require overnight on the West Coast. Could be a splurge helicopter glacier hike option from Queenstown.

### Wānaka — Day Trip from Queenstown (confirmed)
45 min from Queenstown. Confirmed as a day trip (not overnight) — drive over early for Roys Peak hike (free, 5-6h, 1,578m), then Rippon vineyard tasting in the afternoon, back to Queenstown for dinner. Lime Tree Lodge (~$220–300/night) is available if an overnight is preferred, but the day trip fits the 14-day frame better.

### Seasonal Pricing: March vs. April
Both months are New Zealand's shoulder season — noticeably cheaper than peak summer (December–February) and before the slow winter (May–August).

| Factor | March | April |
|---|---|---|
| General pricing | Shoulder season, but busier weekends linger from summer | Slightly lower — typically 5–15% cheaper than March |
| Queenstown | Still busy (school holiday influx fades mid-March) | Quieter, better availability, autumn colour bonus |
| Tongariro Crossing | Ideal weather window — warm, dry | Good but slightly cooler — still excellent |
| Easter impact | Easter 2027 date TBD — if in April, rates spike on that weekend | Avoid Easter weekend (check 2027 dates — April 1 is Easter 2027) |
| Wānaka | Autumn colour peaks in April — stunning for Roys Peak | Peak for autumn foliage viewing |
| **Recommendation** | Late March (after ~Mar 20) is ideal | Early April (pre-Easter) is strong — April 2027 Easter falls April 1 (Good Fri Mar 28 – Easter Sun Apr 1) — book mid-April onward |

**Note on Easter 2027:** Easter Sunday falls on April 1, 2027. Good Friday is March 28. The long weekend (Mar 27–Apr 1) will see elevated prices and booking pressure across all South Island stops. Aim to be in transit or at a pre-booked property over that weekend — do not leave accommodation bookings for that weekend until late.
